## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small trade and construction businesses (Handwerk und Bau, typically 3 to 20 employees): electricians, plumbing and heating, roofers, painters, carpenters, landscapers and similar. They run the business themselves, read on the phone between jobs, and decide alone. Their job: get a website that brings inquiries and job applications without spending their own time on it. Many already have an outdated site; a few have none.

## Product Purpose

Pixelgesellen builds websites for these businesses at a fixed price. The owner gives about one hour (first call and a 30-minute interview); Pixelgesellen writes the texts, designs, builds and launches the site in 14 days. Success is a site that is found on Google, works on the phone, and produces inquiries and applications.

## Positioning

A two-person service specialised in trades, not a generic web agency: fixed prices instead of price ranges, the price visible before any conversation, texts written from an interview, a careers page for hiring, and the Google Business Profile set up. Buy only: a one-off price with an optional care plan. A rental model is postponed until about 20 sites are sold, because it puts the pre-financing and the default risk on Pixelgesellen.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from the homepage, Google, printed letters sent to businesses, and later social media. They compare against builders (Wix, Jimdo), freelancers and other fixed-price offers, often on the phone. The next step after deciding is a free 15-minute call booked through cal.com, or a message through the contact form; email is the fallback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Packages, confirmed 2026-11-02: sizes S 990 €, M 1,690 €, L 2,990 €, net, plus statutory VAT. Care plan per size 29 / 39 / 59 € per month, cancellable monthly. No rental for now (decided 2026-11-02). Two correction rounds in every package. L includes one time-saving feature; further features and extra pages are priced individually as add-ons. The first five clients get 30 % off the one-off price in return for a review and permission to show the site as a reference.
- Delivery: online in 14 days from the interview.
- Photos are not included; Pixelgesellen recommends a local photographer.
- Legal texts come from a generator or the client's lawyer; Pixelgesellen does not give legal advice.
- The main site serves clients nationwide and remotely. A regional page for Berlin and the surrounding area is reserved at `/berlin`.

## Product Principles

- The price is never a secret: every package and add-on has a fixed number, shown before contact.
- The owner's time is the scarcest resource; every step is designed around one hour of their involvement.
- Trades language over agency language.
- Honest about what is not included.
